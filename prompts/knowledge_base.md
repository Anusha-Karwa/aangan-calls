## What we do

Aangan Studio offers end-to-end interior design for residential and small commercial spaces across Pune and PCMC.

- Space planning and layout optimisation
- Material selection (flooring, wall finishes, ceilings)
- Furniture design and curation (custom + sourced)
- Lighting design and specification
- Kitchen and wardrobe design
- Execution supervision with our own network of contractors and vendors

**Residential:** apartments, independent houses, villas. Full home, specific floors, or individual rooms where the scope is a complete redesign with execution.

**Commercial:** offices, clinics, studios, from about 500 up to approximately 3,000 sq ft. Includes workstations, cabins, reception and common areas.

## What we don't do

- Architecture and structural work: we don't move walls, modify the structure, or handle permits.
- Decor, styling or colour advice only: our minimum engagement is a room redesign with execution.
- Standalone furniture sourcing without a design project.
- Standalone Vastu consultation (we do incorporate Vastu into our designs).
- Restaurants, hotels, retail stores, gyms.
- Projects outside Pune city and PCMC.

## Service area

Pune city: Kothrud, Baner, Aundh, Wakad, Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar, Magarpatta, NIBM, Kondhwa, Undri, Shivane, Warje, Erandwane, Deccan, Kharadi, Nanded City, Sus, Bavdhan, Pashan and adjoining areas.
PCMC: Pimpri, Chinchwad, Pimple Saudagar, Pimple Nilakh, Ravet, Hinjewadi.
Not served: Talegaon, Lonavala, Nashik, Mumbai, or other cities. (Reason: no vendor network there.)

## Typical scope

- Full home: 2BHK onwards, end-to-end design and execution.
- Partial home: a full floor, or 2+ rooms with execution.
- Office: up to ~3,000 sq ft.
- Single room: bedroom or living room, complete redesign with materials, furniture and execution. Not just advice.

## Timelines

- Design phase: 3–4 weeks from the first consultation.
- Execution: 8–16 weeks depending on size and site readiness.
- We cannot take a project that must be ready in under 6 weeks from today.

## Consultation

- Free, 60 minutes, with a designer, no obligation.
- Either a site visit (designer comes to the property) or a studio visit.
- Useful to bring: floor plan or rough sizes, photos of the space, reference images, and everyone who will decide.

## What does NOT disqualify a caller

Not knowing exactly what they want; calling outside office hours; asking about price; being unsure about materials or style; a single-room project with full execution; a rented flat (no structural changes).

## Pricing questions

Never give any number, range, per-sq-ft rate or "starts at". The only answer:

"Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope — your designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can book that for you right now if you'd like."
